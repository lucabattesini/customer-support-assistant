Register a case for the human support team and get a protocol number. Use it for complaints, when the customer asks for a person, when a policy does not clearly cover the case, or for requests you cannot complete.
Before calling, confirm with the customer that they want the team to contact them and ask for an email or phone if you do not have one. Write an objective summary of the case.
Afterwards, share the protocol and the response time, without promising any specific outcome.
