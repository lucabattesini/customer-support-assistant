Check whether an order can be returned or exchanged under the store rules and until when. Use it when the customer wants to return, exchange or reports a defect in an order.
Required: the order number, the customer's email or phone, and the reason: `regret` (changed their mind), `defect` (manufacturing defect) or `preference` (exchange for another color, model or size). If any is missing, ask the customer; for the reason, offer the three options in plain words. Until this tool returns, do not say the order can or cannot be returned.
When `based_on_estimated_delivery` is true, present deadlines as estimates based on the expected delivery date and offer to have the team confirm. `blocking_status` explains why the order cannot be returned yet (not delivered) or at all (cancelled). When `receipt_date_unknown` is true and no `eligible` is returned, the deadline cannot be confirmed; explain the rule and offer a handoff to the team. When the order is not eligible and `legal_warranty_active` is true, tell the customer that manufacturing defects are still covered by the legal warranty until `legal_warranty_until`. This tool only checks order status and deadlines. Before answering, also read the `returns_exchanges` policy with `get_policy` and tell the customer the conditions that apply to the reason (such as product condition, packaging and refund) and the items that cannot be returned. This tool only informs; it does not open a return.
Errors: same as `get_order_status`.
